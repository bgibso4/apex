Structure
Outer frame flush with the regular card edges, member cards inset 8px. One member expanded at a time, and it is the same card anatomy as any other exercise. Logging its set for the round collapses it and expands the next. The inactive member cards are jump targets, not log targets.
One subheader line (21d)
Target then history on a single line, in reading order, with weight doing the separating: 12px/700 mid grey for what is being asked, 11px/600 dim for what happened. Both RPE numbers stay, because today's cap and last session's achieved rating are different facts. The name row stays clear for the swapped pill and the rest countdown, and on a long percentage prescription the line wraps into exactly the two line arrangement it replaced.
Round and rest share the slot
The round is named once, in the group header, and the countdown appears beside it rather than replacing it, so the round never goes missing mid session. There is no rest state: the next round is already open behind the timer. Tap the countdown to clear it.
Effort per exercise
Each member is rated on its own last set, in its own card, so the three ratings arrive one per member turn through the final round instead of stacking at the end. No group rating on top: three numbers you can compare next session beat one that averages them. Unrated stays a real state, so tapping the next exercise moves on and records nothing.
Why 19a over 19d
19d was quieter collapsed, but once a member opened it read as a thinner version of the card it was replacing: same content, weaker separation, and a band that had to keep inventing contrast the card already had. 9d stands unchanged, and the app keeps one card anatomy instead of two. The audit cuts and the rest and effort behaviour were never dependent on that choice, so they carry over intact.
