# APEX Design System

Dark, precise, iOS. Two hues on a neutral field. If a screen disagrees with `foundations/`, the screen is wrong.

## Files

- `foundations/` colors, typography, spacing (source of truth)
- `context/` briefs, research, design-language history
- `references/` art-direction rounds (history only)
- `screens/` approved screens to fork from

## Color

- **Accent #659BC8** = now. **Moss #6CA383** = earned. No third hue, ever. Errors and misses are neutral text.
- Neutrals: field #090A0C / #0C0E11 · card #14161B → #0E1013 · border #20232A, top edge #2C3039 · text #EDEEF0 / #8F9098 / #7C7E86 (muted; was #55575F, raised for 4.5:1 contrast, iOS 27 pass) · on-accent #0B0C0E
- Tints: 10% alpha of the hue (#659BC81A, #6CA3831A)
- Light: halo `radial-gradient(ellipse 90% 45% at 50% -5%, #141A22, #0C0E11 55%, #090A0C)` behind the header; cards use the vertical gradient + lighter top edge. No shadows.
- Earned cards only: moss tint wash at the top, \`radial-gradient(ellipse 80% 60% at 50% 0%, rgba(108,163,131,0.13), transparent 70%)\` over the card gradient, border moss at \~40%.
- Glow: static glow only on earned cards. One brief pulse may announce something newly due: it plays once on arrival (900ms, 0 → 8px ring at the hue's 60% alpha), then stops. Nothing else glows. One addition (owner 2026-11-24): when Workout complete arrives, a point of moss light runs one lap around the earned card's edge, once (1.5s).

## Type

System stack (SF). Wordmark font is the only exception.

- Hero numeral 34/800, -0.5px · Tab title 28/800 (Home's wordmark 34) · Screen title 17/700 (pushed screens and sheets, iOS standard inline size) · Card name 14/700 · Value 13/600 · Body 11/400 #8F9098 · Eyebrow 11/700 uppercase +0.6px #7C7E86 (section labels too, on every screen; a note at the right of a section label is uppercase in the same style, Program summary turn 11 r1b and r2b, owner 2026-11-25) · Minimum text size 11px everywhere (Apple minimum); tab bar labels stay system; graph labels are the exception and stay small: block names in a graph's strip 9.5px, months and scale numbers 10px (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 50 r1c: "The one I dont think we should move are the graph related ones")
- Named sizes in use, part of the scale (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 50 r4a: "Standardizing the fonts is key to this design system"): Record large 30/800 -0.5px (latest record on Rep records; Home's recovery number is also 30) · Readout 24/800 -0.3px (a graph card's number) · Tile day 18/700 (the day in a date tile) · Record value 15/700 (record weights, Best, the right-hand number on Sessions rows) · Helper 12/500 #8F9098 (lines under a sheet's fields). 12px text is 12/500 in any colour (owner 2026-11-25, Program summary turn 11 r7b: "both should use the correct scale"; built on Program summary and Exercise detail).
- Numerals always tabular. Biggest number wins; hierarchy by size and weight, never color.

## Shape and spacing

- Radius: card 10 · nested 6 · pill 999
- Screen padding 20 · card padding 14×16 · card gap 12 (every card stacked directly on another card, on every screen; section labels keep their own spacing; owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 51 r51b: "unify at 12"; tiles side by side in a grid are 12pt apart both ways, Progress turn 29 r1b) · section label 8 above its card, on every screen (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 52: "Why not let's just do eight points everywhere") · section gap 24 · tap target ≥44

## Actions

**Primary**, filled, one per card max, fill by meaning:

- **Blue** #659BC8, 50px, 15/700, text #0B0C0E. The thing to do now.
- **Moss** #6CA383, 50px. Confirms something earned. Only when a real case needs it.
- **Tonal** rgba(237,238,240,0.06), 44px, 14/600 #EDEEF0, no border. Lesser action alone on a card.

**Secondary**: text 14/600 #8F9098, 44px tap row, centered. Only directly under a primary.

Rules: one primary per card · lone lesser action → Tonal · whole card tappable → no button · no chevrons on buttons or secondary text.
- A button that changes state in place keeps its size (owner 2026-11-23): same height, corners and type in every state; only the fill and text colour change. Example: Continue / Finish workout, quiet then blue.

## Screen header

* Large titles (owner 2026-11-24; amended 2026-11-25, Home turn 37c): every screen with a large title uses Apple's standard large-title navigation bar. The bar buttons have a row of their own at the top (44px); the large title sits on the row under it (owner: "Apple's standard is to have it strictly below"), scrolls away with the content, and the title then shows small (17/700), centered on the buttons' row; no custom shrink in place. The subheader scrolls away with the large title and does not come back under the small one. Content scrolls under a soft blurred edge, no hard bar line. Home's wordmark: 34px (Apple's large-title size, our hero numeral size) on its own row under the gear, then 17px centered on the gear's row; built into the Home Demo. Every tab has the same layout (owner 2026-11-25, Workout turns 22a and 23a): the top row is kept for buttons even when a tab has none, the title sits on its own row under it at the same height as Home's wordmark, and only Home's wordmark is 34px; every other tab's title is 28/800 (Apple's Title 1). Built into the Workout and Progress tabs. This replaces the earlier pattern (wordmark shrinking in place at the left), which was chosen before the owner knew the standard.
* Bar buttons: 44px glass circle (system glass material, follows the user's transparency setting), fixed top right.

* Content top (app-wide token, owner 2026-11-09; reaffirmed 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 50 r3b: "Make this consistent across the app. No screen should be exempt unless very explicitly overriden"): the first content starts 24px below the nav bar on every screen (128px from the top of a 390 × 844 screen). Pushed screens open with it there, on their small title. The one explicit override: tabs open on their large title and start content under it (Home 37c, Workout 22a and 23a). Content scrolls under the bar; the bar title stays put.
* Pushed screens (Calendar, History, Program summary): the bar buttons sit where iOS puts the navigation bar, 59-103pt, the same height as Home's gear (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 44a: "I want iOS standard for this"). 44px glass back circle on the left, title centered at 17/700 on the buttons' row, at most one 44px glass action on the right (History, Filter). Icon only, no text on bar buttons. Every pushed screen has a large title, and opens on the small one: the small title (17/700, at most two lines, then …) sits between the buttons, and pulling down brings in the large title (28/800) above the content, as Apple's own apps do (owner 2026-11-25, Header Audit Demo H1: "I think option one is probably what we want"; locked: "Yeah, I think you can lock all three"). While the page sits where it opened the bar has no soft edge; it comes in once content scrolls under the bar. Tabs keep opening on their large title. Exercise detail: the sub header goes with the large title and the period stepper stays in view in the content (Exercise turn 48a). A subtitle under the small title (Apple's Passwords style) is an option to consider later for a screen or two.

## Liquid Glass (iOS 27)

Added 2026-11-07 from the Home, Calendar, History and Program summary sessions. Build with the system materials (`TabView`, glass effect) so the iOS 27 transparency setting (clear → tinted) applies; the values below are how the mocks draw it.

- **Glass recipe, controls:** `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.14), rgba(255,255,255,0.05))`, border 1px `rgba(255,255,255,0.16)`, inset top highlight white 34%, inset bottom shadow black 32%, outer edge 1px black 55%, blur 20–24 + saturate 1.6–1.8. Bar buttons, sheet close buttons.
- **Tab bar:** floating capsule, 62px tall, 20px in from the screen edges, 26px above the bottom, flat glass fill `rgba(28,30,36,0.62)` (no top-to-bottom gradient), 1px white 12% border. Four tabs: filled symbol over a 10/600 sentence-case label. Selected tab sits on a lighter lens (white 11%), icon and label accent; others #EDEEF0. Content scrolls under it with a short soft fade (92px, field at 70% → 0). Home indicator light, below the bar.
- **Sheets:** large detent glass sheet (stops under the status bar), grabber, 44px glass close top left (HIG: Close/Cancel leading, Done trailing), title centered 17/700; primary at the foot is a capsule (concentric with the sheet corners), content dimmed behind, swipe down or tap outside to close. Primary at the foot, secondary text under it.
- Content cards stay painted light (the card gradient), never glass.

## Forms and editing

* Pickers (app-wide, owner 2026-11-09): one picker per kind of value, the same everywhere. Dates use the graphical calendar; times and durations use wheels. The value shows in a grey pill (white text), blue while its picker is open. Never a different date or time picker on another screen. Where the picker appears (inline under its row, as in Session detail edit, or elsewhere) is decided per screen.
* Deletes (app-wide, owner 2026-11-09): deleting something with contents (an exercise, a session) asks to confirm with the standard iOS confirmation dialog; iOS draws its destructive button in system red, the one place red appears. A single row removed by swipe › trash (a set) needs no dialog and no Undo: it happens in edit mode, where closing without saving brings it back (owner 2026-11-25, Session Detail turn 14).
* Alerts and confirmations (app-wide, owner 2026-11-26): always Apple's own system alert or confirmation dialog (SwiftUI `.alert` / `.confirmationDialog`), in the system's default glass look; never a custom-drawn popup. The mocks only approximate it. (Owner: "make sure this is apples default glass popup … Not trying to roll our own popup.")
* Toasts (app-wide, owner 2026-11-10): used only for Undo after removing a single row outside an edit mode (a removed link). No "Saved", "Discarded" or "Added" toasts; the screen showing the result is the confirmation.
* Menus (app-wide, owner 2026-11-18): one pull-down menu everywhere, the iOS system menu (SwiftUI `Menu`): glass panel 220pt wide, radius 22, 46pt rows, 16pt text, hairline dividers between groups, scales in from its corner. Action menus (Session detail •••) put the icon on the right of each row; pick-one menus (Rep records filter) put a check left of the current pick. Opened from a 44pt glass bar button (•••) or, inside content, a 36pt tonal capsule (14/600 label + chevron, 44pt tap area) showing the current pick.
* Editing an exercise (owner 2026-11-21): Exercise detail has a glass ••• at the top right opening the standard menu (Edit exercise; Archive joins it once All exercises is designed). Edit exercise is a sheet only as tall as its fields: Name, Body part (pills, any number), Equipment (one pick, from the menu), Lift type, Progression step (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 35b: segmented 2.5 · 5 · 10 lb, 5 lb by default, seconds for a timed exercise, 5 · 10 · 15 sec, 5 sec by default; what the workout's Felt easy adds and Felt hard takes off), Note. Lift type (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turns 36b and 37a): Automatic · Main lift · Accessory, Automatic by default. Every exercise has a default type: the big barbell lifts (squat, bench, deadlift, overhead press and close variants) are main lifts; everything else, and any custom exercise, an accessory. Automatic follows the running program when it includes the lift ("Pillars treats this as a main lift."), otherwise the exercise's default ("A main lift by default."); Main lift or Accessory overrides it in every program ("Always a main lift, whatever the program."). Lift type decides the workout's progression prompt (accessories only) and Exercise detail's graph (est. 1RM or weight). Tapping a text field raises the sheet to full height with the keyboard, so no field is ever under it. Under the screen title, one quiet line shows the tags and equipment (Quads · Glutes · Barbell). Exercises are keyed by ID, so a rename keeps all history; there is no Delete. The note shows in a Note card above Resources (no note, no card).
* Keyboard (app-wide, owner 2026-11-09): numbers use the system number pad (decimal pad for weight). A tapped field always scrolls into the visible area above the keyboard, never under it. The bar above the keys (previous, next, field name, Done) is the standard SwiftUI keyboard toolbar.

## Charts

- Bars are grey; hue is not used to encode series. Two series: light grey (#A4A5AC) and dark grey (#70727A, raised from #5E6069 to clear 3:1 against the #20232A track), legend above the chart.
- Give quantities a scale: faint lines and labels at 0, 50, 100 for percentages. Values with different units never share an axis; put the odd one out as a number column (e.g. strain beside recovery and sleep).
- Repeating groups (blocks, weeks) stack as rows so a longer program adds height, not width. Labels sit above their bars with the full row; bars keep the full width whatever the label.
- Weekly charts (Progress, owner 2026-11-12): a dashed line at the range average; deload weeks a darker bar; the current week an outline that fills, labeled "so far"; averages count training weeks only. Weekly up to 6 months; monthly on 1 year; longer histories step to quarters past 2 years and half-years past 6. RPE is a line on a 5–10 scale, not bars.
- Session trend lines (Progress): a rolling median of 3 sessions; under 8 sessions, no smoothing (a tunable constant).
- Est. 1RM graph (Exercise detail, owner 2026-11-17): the smoothed est. 1RM line plus a best-so-far staircase with a moss dot at each PR; no numbers on the graph; the legend (Est. 1RM · Best so far · PR) sits under it, centred as a group. PR dots closer than 7px merge. A range with no PR draws the best as a dashed line, no label. Drag to read: the readout above the graph shows that session (at rest, Now and Best); near a PR dot it snaps to that PR. **Snap distance still to settle on device:** 6pt in the mocks (12pt felt too sensitive).
- Program blocks on the est. 1RM graph (owner 2026-11-17): a thin strip between the graph and the months, one segment per block in greys by type (deload nearly blank), the name only when the whole word fits; a wider gap starts a new program. Over 10 blocks in the range (tunable), the strip shows programs instead. While dragging, the block (or program) under the finger lights up as a faint column over the graph, its strip segment is outlined, and the readout names program · block.
- Accessory graph (Exercise detail, owner 2026-11-21): for an accessory the graph card is Weight, not est. 1RM. A step line shows the weight used (it moves only when the weight goes up) and one grey bar per session under it shows the average reps per set; no PR dots, no best-so-far line, and the corner shows the change in lb. Press and drag picks the nearest session: its bar turns white, a rule and dot mark it on the weight line, its block (or program, past 10 blocks) lights up as on a main lift, and the readout shows that day's weight, block, sets and RPE. On long ranges the bars become one per month showing that month's average reps (the readout gives sessions, average reps and average RPE); the weight line is never averaged. Owner: monthly averages are a weak signal, kept for lack of a better choice. The switch is threshold based (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 38a): one bar per session up to 45 sessions in range, one per month past that, so the same range can differ between exercises. Past 45 months, one bar per quarter, then one per half-year past 45 quarters, the same steps as Progress's weekly charts (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 39a); never more than 45 bars. The weight line has the space from the top of the graph down to just above the tallest reps bar, and the graph stays a main lift's height (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 41a). Behind the weight line, faint even lines at round weights with no labels: no numbers on accessory graphs, while a main lift's graph keeps its labelled scale (owner 2026-11-25, Exercise turn 40b). No snapping or haptic. Everything below the graph is the same as a main lift.

## Motion

- Month flip: slide by direction, 28px, 260ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).
- Content swap in place (a day card): fade in, 240ms, ease-out.
- Switching tabs (owner 2026-11-25, Motion Explorations 4a): the iOS tab bar's own behaviour. The new tab's screen is there at once, where you left it; only the glass highlight slides to the picked tab. No crossfade, no slide.
- Timings (app-wide, locked 2026-11-09): quick 150–200ms for color and state; smooth 300–350ms spring, no bounce, for anything that moves or resizes; snappy ~300ms with a small bounce only for things the person places or ticks. Content arriving and text never bounce. Accessibility settings (Reduce Motion, Dynamic Type and the like) are out of scope; the app is personal (owner 2026-11-25: "don't worry about reduced motion. This app, we're not really gonna make this app super accessible. We're not gonna worry about that right now. I actually don't really care. - Haptics (app-wide, locked 2026-11-09), Apple's meanings, kept rare: success = saving an edit, saving the check-in, finishing a workout, a new PR · light impact = a set deleted by swipe, Add set, an exercise returning on Undo · selection = ticking a protocol, History quick pills and filter options, check-in slider steps (pickers, wheels, calendar play their own) · none = navigation, scrolling, sheets, menus, typing, toasts, charts, pressing cards (one exception: a subtle haptic when the est. 1RM readout snaps to a PR, owner 2026-11-16; check strength on device); destructive dialogs use only the system's.
- Workout flow (Motion Explorations turn 2, owner 2026-11-24).
