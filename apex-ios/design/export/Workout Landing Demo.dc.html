What is in here (all locked)
The week strip sits in the day card and shows this week only. A circle is that day's session: ticked when that session is done, whatever day you did it; dashed when its day has passed and it isn't done; a plain number is a rest day. The card states the real date when it differs ("Done Tue, Aug 18"). Buttons by state: Start Workout on today's session, Do this today on any other unfinished session this week, View session on a done one. Nothing moves when you catch up. A rest day keeps its own quiet card. Anything else logged on a day sits straight under the card, above the preview or What you did (Logged today, Logged that day, Also logged today). An unfinished session previews its warm-up, exercises (the RPE cap and any percentage under the amount) and post-workout. A superset stays inside the Exercises card: a Superset / rounds label over its rows, joined by a line down their left side. The title is Apple's standard large title: it scrolls away with the page and comes back small and centred in the bar; the program line scrolls away with it. A done session shows a short What you did with PRs tagged. With no program the page is Home's No Active Program card.
Not in this demo
Start Workout and Done play their transitions once the flow is in the whole-app demo (they cross from this page to the session). The header is a drawing of Apple's large title; the build uses the system's own. A session row under Logged that day or Also logged today opens the Session Detail Demo; the Run row doesn't open anything (the Run tab isn't designed). Looking at other weeks lives on Calendar.
