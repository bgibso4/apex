Locked
Tabs for Warm-up · Exercises · Post-workout, which scroll away with the page. Nothing above the tabs changes when you switch: one header and one ⋯ menu, and its Add exercise and Reorder always work on the exercise list. Continue on every tab, Finish workout on the last. Buttons at the foot only: grey, then blue, and Finish workout green once every set of every exercise is logged. The Warm-up and Post-workout tabs; protocol rows open on a tap. Round set ticks. Add exercise and Reorder, both from ⋯ on any tab (Add exercise: the filter button top right opens Body part · Equipment · Lift type rollers and Sort; one order at a time, Recent first, A to Z with letter labels, or Most done; opens on all body parts) (in Reorder, swipe a row you added to remove it; ✕ undoes everything). Swap and Note, on the exercise card. Timed sets (start, stop, tick; only Rest is labelled on the clock; "Felt easy. Go to 1:00?" on a set time, nothing when you go to failure). The set column at 13pt. The time editor: tap a logged time. The Workout complete screen after Finish workout (Jump to has the session as the rest of the app shows it, and one with an exercise added, swapped and skipped; Done restarts the demo here, and View session isn't wired). The RPE row with its number at the size of the weights. Detail · Swap · Note, the ✓ and ✕ on the prompt, the set editor and the skip sheet. The logging itself (cards, rest countdown, effort slider, superset) is your earlier round's.
Still open
Detail opens the exercise's page over the workout (a stand-in here) and Back returns you where you were. The timed prompt adds the exercise's progression step, 5 sec by default. Demo limits: search isn't wired up in Add exercise or Swap, Swap and Note only open from the Barbell Row card, and only Backspace works on the drawn keyboard. In the superset, a logged hold can’t be edited here (on its own card, tap the time), and the bodyweight member gets no “Felt easy” prompt (your rule). The prompt needs every set at its target reps or its set time, and a rating under the cap; the “drop to” prompt for two rough sessions in a row isn’t drawn yet.
